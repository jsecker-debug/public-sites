Privacy Policy

The short version

  • Your library lives on your device and in your own iCloud account. We can't see it.
  • No Yomi accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell data.
  • Your voice is transcribed on your device and never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • A question goes to an AI provider only after you've agreed, and you can turn that off at any time.

Who we are

Yomi is a reading companion app for iPhone and iPad published by Joshua Secker ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy, email js.apps.support@gmail.com.

Information that stays with you

Your library. Books, reading progress, sessions, notes, commentary, quotes, reviews, rankings and goals are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they sync through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your voice. During a reading session Yomi uses your microphone and Apple's on-device speech recognition to hear requests. Transcription happens on your device. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Your camera. The camera is used to scan book barcodes and to capture passages from a page. Text is recognised on your device. Images aren't stored or sent anywhere.

Settings. Your preferences are stored on your device.

Widgets and Live Activities. Widgets show your current book from a copy stored on your device. The Lock Screen Live Activity can show the words of your requests; you can turn this off in Settings › Reading companion › Show requests on the Lock Screen.

AI answers (Anthropic)

The first time Yomi would send a question to an AI service, it asks your permission and lists what is sent. If you allow it, each question you ask the companion is sent to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the Claude models that write the answer. Along with the question, Yomi sends:

  • the book's title and author
  • where you're up to (page, chapter and percentage)
  • your recent notes and commentary on that book
  • earlier questions and answers from the same session, so follow-ups make sense

Yomi doesn't send your name, email, Apple Account or any device identifier with these requests. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API data isn't used to train its models, and it may be kept for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

If you choose "Not now", or turn off "Send questions to Claude" in Settings › Reading companion, nothing is sent and Yomi answers on your device where Apple Intelligence is available.

Natural voices (OpenAI)

If you're a Premium subscriber and choose a natural voice, and you've agreed to it, the text of Yomi's spoken answers is sent to OpenAI to turn it into audio. Only the answer text is sent: never your voice, your question or your library. Turn off "Send answers to OpenAI" to use the system voice instead. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Our server

Premium AI requests pass through a small server we run on Cloudflare. It checks your subscription, forwards your request to Anthropic or OpenAI and streams the reply back. It does not store the text of your questions or answers. It keeps only:

  • an identifier for your subscription taken from the signed receipt Apple provides (not linked to your name, email or Apple Account)
  • your subscription status and expiry
  • monthly usage counts, used to apply fair-use limits
  • short-lived technical logs (for example, errors and request timings) without question or answer text, kept for 30 days

Cloudflare processes this on our behalf. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Book search

When you search for a book or scan a barcode, the search text or ISBN is sent to Open Library (Internet Archive) and Google Books to find editions and covers, and cover images are downloaded from them. They receive an ordinary web request from your device, including your IP address. See the Internet Archive's terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app, and our server, which subscription you have and when it renews or expires.

Legal basis (UK and EU)

We rely on your consent to send questions and answers to AI providers (you can withdraw it at any time in Settings), and on performance of a contract to provide Premium and apply fair-use limits.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete a book in the app, or delete Yomi and remove its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Yomi.
  • Our server keeps subscription records while the subscription is active plus 12 months, and usage counts for the current and previous month.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, to object, and to data portability. Because we don't hold your library and don't link server records to you, most requests can be handled on your device. For anything else, email js.apps.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

Yomi isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change what Yomi sends or who receives it, we'll update this page and change the effective date above, and the app will ask for your permission again before sending anything new.

Help

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email js.apps.support@gmail.com

In the app, Settings → Help → Email support fills in your app and iOS version. Send diagnostics also attaches the crash reports iOS keeps for Yomi, never your books, notes or questions.

How do I talk to Yomi while I read?

Start a reading session from a book, then say "Yomi" followed by your question, a note or a page number. You can turn off the need to say "Yomi" first in Settings → Reading companion.

How do I cancel Yomi Premium?

Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Yomi. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I subscribed on another device.

In Yomi, open Settings → Yomi Premium and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My library isn't syncing between devices.

Check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud is allowed for Yomi in iOS Settings. Yomi's Settings shows the reason if a sync fails.

What gets sent to AI services?

Only what you agree to. See the privacy policy above.
